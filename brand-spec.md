## Observed Posture

- Clean journal navigation with a compact lowercase wordmark and simple top links.
- Large centered identity moment followed by a personal intro and author block.
- Alternating full-width post rows: image on one side, text on the other, with occasional dark charcoal bands.
- Restrained color budget: mostly white, charcoal, light grey, and a muted blue accent.
- Soft image-led editorial rhythm, minimal radii, modest shadows, and spacious vertical padding.
